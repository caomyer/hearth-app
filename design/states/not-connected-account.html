Design notes

  1. The known part, labelled with what it leaves out. Today one unknown balance turns the whole hero into "Not known yet", which is right for an account about to sync and wrong for one hearth will never be told about. This reuses the known-part presentation from a withdrawn net worth, with the dashed grey chip of "not there yet" instead of the violet of "withheld".
  2. The label carries the gap too. "Without 3 accounts hearth does not see" is in the figure's own label, so a screenshot of the number alone still says it is partial.
  3. Accounts hearth does not see sit below the total, in their own group. The total row says "without the 3 below", so the table adds up exactly and never puts a "Not known" above a sum.
  4. An account page shows what moved, never a balance made of it. "In from your connected accounts" is a flow with its dates, and the sentence under it says why it is not what the account holds. Summing those rows into a balance is the exact mistake of the rejected Outside bucket.
  5. Connecting stays a quiet action. No badge, no reminder: an account a member chooses never to connect is a normal state, not a task.
  6. Another member sees that the figure is partial, and nothing else unless they may see Jordan's balances: no count, no names, the same words whether one account is left out or five.
  7. The history chart (not drawn here) keeps drawing the connected accounts, with "without 3 accounts hearth does not see" in its legend, instead of disappearing as it does for an account whose history has not arrived.
