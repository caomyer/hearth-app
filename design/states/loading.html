Design notes

  1. Skeletons in the shape of the real content. Headings, labels and chart titles are known before any figure is, so they render immediately; only the numbers wait. When the data lands, nothing moves.
  2. A calm shimmer, not a spinner. It is suppressed entirely for anyone who prefers reduced motion.
  3. Screen readers hear one sentence ("Loading household figures") instead of a dozen empty shapes, which are hidden from assistive technology.
  4. Refreshing is not loading. When figures already exist, they stay on screen at reduced emphasis with their as-of time, rather than flashing back to skeletons. A number that disappears is more alarming than a number that is a few hours old.
