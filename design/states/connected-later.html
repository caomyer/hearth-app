Design notes

  1. The account kept by name is the first target offered, matched by institution and last digits, so connecting it later continues the same account: same name, same owners, same history of moves.
  2. Owners are not offered for change here. The account already has owners; changing them is a separate, audited act on the account itself, never a side effect of connecting a bank.
  3. The page promises the one thing people fear: what you told hearth is checked, not added again. Without matching, each move would count twice and the bank's balance check would fail; the import says, in counts, how every one was resolved.
  4. Three honest outcomes, and no fourth. Matched (counted once), before the bank's history (inside its opening balance), or not in its records (back in Review). Nothing stays on the account on the member's word once the bank's own word is available.
  5. Before and after, side by side. Net worth rises by exactly the two balances the bank reports, and the dashed chip shrinks to the one account still not connected.
  6. The stepper is the connect flow's own, three steps, since the member is not new; the onboarding stepper's admin step does not apply.
