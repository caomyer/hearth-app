Design notes

  1. Zero is an outcome, so it says what it means. Not "No items": all caught up, when the next import is, and why nothing needed you this morning.
  2. The durable-decision promise is made visible. "All 14 matched decisions you had already made" is the product's core idea, proven on the day it works.
  3. Recent decisions stay reachable. A bulk accept is a big click; an undo list for a week makes it safe to use, and shows overrides ("not the suggested Shopping") so a wrong suggestion is visible after the fact.
  4. The badge disappears from the navigation rather than showing "0".
