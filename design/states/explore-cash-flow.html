Design notes

  1. Cash flow is the second-ranked question and gets its own starter question, "Are we saving anything?", on Household and Explore.
  2. In, out and left over always cover the same people. Money in without Jordan, less money out with Jordan, would be a number nobody can interpret. A part enters this view only if it shares both totals.
  3. Saving is not spending. A move to a savings or brokerage account is a transfer between accounts (docs/books.md), so it lands in "left over" and the footer says so in one sentence a non-accountant can read.
  4. Sources are transactions. An employer's name is what a transaction says, so it is shown only for parts whose transactions the viewer may see. Sam's money in is one row with its total.
