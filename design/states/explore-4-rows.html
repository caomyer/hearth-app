Design notes

  1. Every drill ends at the payments. fava ends a drill at the journal; so does hearth, but only for payments the viewer may see. For anyone else, the drill ends one step earlier, at the hatched row that carries the amount they may see, and asking for this page by its address answers like a page that does not exist (Concealed).
  2. The end of the path is somewhere to act. Each payment is the viewer's own, so it can be recategorised from here, through the same desk as Review. The footer says what that changes, including in what Sam sees of Alex's categories.
  3. The place's own history answers the next question ("is this new?") without going back up.
  4. The trail keeps every step. Take back "Lantern Noodle Bar" and you are at step 3; take back "October" and you are at Dining over the year.
