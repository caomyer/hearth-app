Design notes

  1. The banner follows its owner everywhere until it is fixed, because an expired connection silently ages every figure it feeds. It appears only for the person who can fix it.
  2. Expired sorts to the top, with its own coverage bar showing where the data stops.
  3. The explanation removes the two fears: it is the bank's routine, not a fault; and reconnecting will not duplicate anything.
  4. Reconnecting is the member's own act. No one else in the household can see or use this connection, including for joint accounts.
  5. The effect on everyone else is spelled out, and it leaks nothing. There is no separate notice of how many accounts are behind or since when, because a count and a day taken from connections would give away the day a member's connection stopped. Each figure says where its data stops instead: to the day for a viewer who may see the transactions behind it, by month for everyone else (docs/privacy-boundary.md).
