Design notes

  1. Stale means older than the last close, not "older than now". On a Sunday, Friday's close is current. Tuesday's is not.
  2. The hero admits it, in one word. "Except one" in the as-of line links straight to the problem; the headline number does not change colour, because almost all of it is fine.
  3. The row says it three ways: an amber row, a clock glyph, and the words "Tue close" and "may be off". Colour alone would fail a colour-blind reader; the words do not.
  4. The size of the risk is stated. "May be off by a few hundred dollars" tells the reader whether to care.
  5. The fix keeps provenance. A hand-entered price is allowed and recorded as such (decision 6), so it never passes as the source's.
