Design notes

  1. Never a smaller number presented as complete. Decision 3's core rule. The label changes ("without Sam"), the missing part is attached to the hero as a hatched chip, and the reconciliation lists Sam as "not included".
  2. The composition bar is removed, not shrunk. A bar of the known parts would draw Alex as a bigger share of the household than they are.
  3. History is consistent. Withdrawing applies to past figures too, so the trend covers the same parts back to the start rather than showing a cliff on the day Sam withdrew.
  4. Withdrawing is never framed as wrong. Violet and a lock, not red and a warning: Sam used a setting as intended.
  5. The disagreement with decision 3 is drawn, not argued. Firstmate can overturn it in one line; nothing else on this page would change.
