Design notes

  1. Coverage stays month-level and the same for every viewer. What changes from today is what the rest of the page does about it. On the real Household page one member's stopped card blanked the household's spending for everyone. Here the parts that are whole keep their figures, by name, and only the total and the part that is not whole read "Not known yet".
  2. Naming whose month is incomplete discloses nothing new. It goes with that part's spending total: Alex's member page already tells Sam that Alex's month's history is incomplete. The day and the reason go only to the owner (may_see_why_unknown).
  3. An outline, never a short bar. October is drawn as an outline at the height of what is known, labelled "Not known", with no value in the tooltip.
  4. No typical before three months, and nothing before the history. Both rules are today's; Explore keeps them on every slice.
