Design notes

  1. An empty page is an introduction, not an apology. One action, said plainly, with the alternative (CSV) beside it for banks that cannot connect.
  2. The outline of what is coming. Three dashed tiles name the sections that will appear and, before there is any data, who will be able to see each one.
  3. Dashed and grey means "not there yet". It is deliberately unlike the violet hatch for "withheld" and the amber for "missing history", so the three absences are never confused.
  4. The household sees a state, not a zero. A new member's net worth is unknown, not $0, so it is never added to the household total as zero.
  5. No review badge. With nothing imported there is nothing to review, and a "0" badge would be noise.
