Design notes

  1. Step 2 of a drill: a category over time. Reached by choosing Dining in the table, or "Dining has been above typical" on Household. The sentence now reads "on Dining", and the trail shows one chip that takes it back.
  2. Stacked by who, because Sam shares categories. Sam's Dining a month is exactly what the category facet shares, so Sam is a full segment. Jordan shares only a total, which cannot be narrowed to Dining, so Jordan is out of this view and the footer says so in fixed words.
  3. By place goes as deep as each part allows. Alex's and the joint accounts' places are listed; Sam's dining is one hatched row with its amount, because the amount is a category figure and the places are transactions. The total stays whole.
  4. The selected month is outlined and links to step 3. The typical line is the middle of the six complete months before October.
