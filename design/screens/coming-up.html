How hearth finds a bill

The same place, at about the same amount, once a month or once a year, at least three times. A bill hearth missed, or one that is not a bill, can be corrected from any of its payments.

Design notes

  1. "What is about to be charged?" is answered from what already happened. No budgets to set up: a bill is a place paid at a steady amount on a steady cadence, found from the viewer's own and joint rows. This is new computation; fixed_categories already does the monthly half of it for categories.
  2. Only accounts the viewer can see into. A bill's day and amount is one transaction, so another member's bills are never inferred or summed here. The footer says so in words that depend on sharing, not on whether they have bills.
  3. "Before your next pay" is the honest part of "can we afford this?". Hearth knows the next expected pay, the bills due before it and the balance of the viewer's own checking account. It does not know what else will be spent, so it does not claim a "safe to spend" figure.
  4. Price rises and stopped bills are changes worth a sentence. They are also on Household under "What changed".
