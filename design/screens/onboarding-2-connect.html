Design notes

  1. The 730-day request is the first thing on the page, before any bank is connected, because it is decided at connection time and cannot be changed after.
  2. Coverage is drawn, not just stated. A two-year bar per institution makes a 90-day connection visibly short in a way "90 days" in a list would not.
  3. Short history warns, never blocks. Decision 2. The warning says whose limitation it is, that reconnecting will not help, and offers CSV upload now or later. Continue stays available.
  4. The gap stays visible afterwards. The warning promises that the card's history is marked as starting in July wherever it appears, so a short window is never silently treated as complete.
  5. Hatched amber marks "missing history". It is a different absence from "withheld" (violet), so it gets its own colour, still with a glyph and words.
