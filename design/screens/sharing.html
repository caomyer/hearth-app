What the household sees of you

These six are the only things that can be shared. Each change applies the moment you make it.

  • Net worth total

    One number. Turning it off makes the household total show as incomplete, never as a smaller number.

  • Monthly spending total

    How much you spent each month. One number a month, nothing about what on.

  • Monthly income total

    How much you earned each month. One number a month, nothing about where from.

  • Spending by category

    Food, transport and so on, per month. No merchants, no individual transactions.

  • Transactions

    Every transaction with its merchant, amount and category.

  • Account balances

    Each account and its balance, and your investment positions.

Joint  Joint accounts are fully visible to both owners. That is not a setting either of you can change alone.

Admins and your data

An admin manages accounts, not other people's money. Being an admin does not let anyone see what you have not shared.

An admin can reset your password, and so could sign in as you. That is the price of a household server without email, so every reset is recorded here and cannot be removed.

Design notes

  1. The preview is the explanation. Each switch changes the card on the right, which is a miniature of the page Sam actually gets. People trust what they can see more than what they are told. Try the switches.
  2. State in words beside every switch. "Shared" and "Not shared" are written out, so the position of a knob is never the only signal.
  3. Defaults match the approved mock: net worth and the spending total on, everything else off. Income defaulted on in an earlier draft; firstmate reversed that on 2026-10-18, since the captain approved the top two shared and everything else private. This screen shows a member who has turned income on.
  4. Income is its own switch. The approved household dashboard shows household income and a savings rate, which need each member's income, so "Monthly income total" sits beside "Monthly spending total" and is controlled separately. No switch covers more than its label says.
  5. The consequence of the sensitive switch is on the switch. Net worth's description says what happens to the household total when it is off (decision 3).
  6. The admin boundary is stated where it matters, with its honest exception. Decisions 1 and 7: no admin override; a reset is possible and always logged, and the log is shown to the person it concerns.
