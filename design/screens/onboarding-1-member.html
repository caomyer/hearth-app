Design notes

  1. The stepper says who does each step. Step 1 is the admin's; steps 2 to 4 are the new member's own, on their first sign-in, because only they should hold their bank connections.
  2. The generated password is readable words. It is handed over in person on a household machine, so it must survive being read aloud. It is displayed once.
  3. Choosing their own password is on by default. Decision 1 accepts that an admin can reset a password; it does not require the admin to keep knowing it. This is a small addition to the approved mock; flagged in rationale.md.
  4. The admin role is described by what it cannot do as well as what it can. Decision 7, stated at the moment someone is made an admin.
  5. The sharing default is stated before the member exists, so nobody is surprised later by what the household could see.
