Design notes

  1. Progress says what is happening and how much is left, in counts people can sanity-check, not a spinner. Each step's result stays on screen when it finishes.
  2. Reconciliation is a visible step. "All 3 accounts match to the cent" is the moment a new member learns why they should trust the numbers.
  3. The handover is honest about scale. Two years is over a thousand transactions. The page says so, and shows why it is not as bad as it sounds: grouped, the largest eight groups are most of it.
  4. Reviewing starts before the import ends. Waiting is the worst part of onboarding; there is no reason to make anyone watch a bar.
  5. Sharing is offered here, as the quiet second action, because this is the first moment the member has data the household could see.
