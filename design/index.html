Hearth design

High-fidelity design for the household finance app. Every screen opens straight from disk: no build step, no server, no network. Every figure and name is invented.

Each screen ends with its design notes. Use the theme switch in the dark strip to check light and dark. Narrow the window, or open on a phone, to see the phone layout.

Design systemType, number formatting, colour with measured contrast, gain and loss, the five kinds of absence, space, motion, the accessibility floor, components and chart rules. RationaleWhat was chosen, what was rejected and why, where decisions were questioned, and the three weakest places in this design.
